UNDERSTAND THE DATA

What are we monitoring?

PM

PM2.5

Fine particles small enough to penetrate deep into the respiratory system.

PM

PM10

Inhalable particles commonly associated with dust, roads and construction activity.

NO₂

Nitrogen Dioxide

A combustion-related pollutant strongly associated with vehicle and industrial emissions.

SO₂

Sulphur Dioxide

A gas mainly associated with the burning of sulphur-containing fuels.

O₃

Ozone

A secondary pollutant formed through chemical reactions involving other pollutants.

CO

Carbon Monoxide

A colourless gas produced mainly through incomplete combustion.

NH₃

Ammonia

A reactive gas that can contribute to the formation of fine particulate matter.

Pb

Lead

A toxic metal that can enter the atmosphere through certain industrial processes.

How does air monitoring work?

Digital air-quality systems transform environmental measurements into information that people can understand.

DATA & METHODOLOGY

Where does the information come from?

AirWise is designed around the workflow used by digital air-quality monitoring systems. Environmental measurements can be collected from monitoring stations, processed digitally and presented through dashboards, charts and alerts.

ABOUT THE PROJECT

AirWise

AirWise is an educational prototype developed to demonstrate the role of digital technology in monitoring, analysing and communicating information about air pollution.

⚠ AirWise is an educational prototype. Demonstration values are used to illustrate the workflow of digital air-quality monitoring and should not be interpreted as current official measurements. The prototype does not directly measure air pollution using the user's device.
